# Simulated research concept illustrations

These six AI-generated concept illustrations were commissioned for the personal website as visual studies inspired by the research topics. They are not reproductions of paper figures or validated experimental results. Public captions and the figure viewer identify their simulated status. Publication metadata is separate from the illustrations.

Each `*-concept.webp` is a lightweight website thumbnail. Each `*-concept-full.webp` retains the source PNG resolution in WebP format (lossless for five images; quality 98 for the larger metabolic-partitioning illustration). Full images are requested when a visitor opens the figure viewer. The original SVG overviews remain in `../architectures/` as historical assets.

The illustrations contain symbolic signals, hypothetical modules and schematic geometry. They should not be read as precise quantitative diagrams or definitive method specifications.
