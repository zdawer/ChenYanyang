  BORA uses an independent probe to choose a replay allocation, fresh formal
  replay to fit the detector, and a correction based on the realized mixture
  to preserve the target risk. The work studies target invariance and evaluates
  adaptive replay on continual fraud detection and a long-tail classification stream.
